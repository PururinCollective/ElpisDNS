ΕΛΠΙΣ Resolver

Most DNS services quietly pass your lookups on to a big provider. We don't. Our own open-source resolver goes straight to the source, checks the signatures on every answer, and keeps nothing afterwards.

  • No upstream provider to trust — it asks the root servers itself
  • Second resolver in the world to check post-quantum ML-DSA-44 signatures
  • Open source, and small enough to run at home
