ECS · AS154516 only

The CDN finds you, not us

A CDN picks its server by looking at who asked, and without help the one asking is the resolver. So YouTube hands back a cache near the resolver, even when there is a better one down the road from you. EDNS Client Subnet, new in ΕΛΠΙΣ Resolver 2.2.0, sends the network part of your address along — a /24 on IPv4, a /56 on IPv6. Never the address itself, and never to the root or TLD servers.

  • On for a.hitoha.moe, b.hitoha.moe and 64.hitoha.moe, the endpoints on Perfect Network.
  • Off everywhere else. If you would rather no subnet left at all, take c.hitoha.moe or gg.hitoha.moe.
  • An answer tailored to a subnet is cached for that subnet alone, and never handed to anyone else.
DNS64 · NAT64

IPv6 only? Still the whole internet

Plenty of VPS plans in the region come with an IPv6 address and no IPv4 at all — and then GitHub, half the package mirrors and a surprising amount of the web are simply out of reach. 64.hitoha.moe answers AAAA for names that only have an A record, pointing into our NAT64, so the traffic leaves over IPv6 and is translated on the way out. Nothing to install on the machine; change the resolver and carry on.

  • Answers from inside South East Asia, on the same network as the ECS endpoints.
  • DoH and DoT, ads and trackers filtered, the same as the everyday resolver. 
# /etc/systemd/resolved.conf
[Resolve]
DNS=2402:4e20:198::64#64.hitoha.moe
DNSOverTLS=yes

And we wrote the resolver

ΕΛΠΙΣ Resolver

The endpoints we operate do not hand the real work to somebody else's software. Behind them sits a recursive resolver written from nothing in portable C99 — one static binary, no dependencies, open source down to the crypto. It is built in the open by the community, with Claude AI as a second pair of hands on the code, and it asks the root, the TLD and the authoritative servers itself.

ML-DSA-44
Second in the world
The post-quantum DNSSEC signature from FIPS 204, validated here and at Cloudflare and almost nowhere else. Signatures gathered today are what somebody forges later; this is the answer given early.
SIMD
Close to the metal
Hand-written AVX2, SSE2 and NEON kernels picked at startup from what the processor reports, so the crypto and the cache lookups run at the speed of the machine rather than the speed of portable C.
Community + Claude
Built in the open
Bug reports from the people running it, fixes argued out in the open, and Claude AI working through the code alongside them. Every change lands in the changelog with the reason it was made.
