Our mission

Why ΕΛΠΙΣ exists

Before your device opens any website, it asks a DNS server where that site lives — hundreds of times a day. Put together, those questions say a lot about you: what you read, where you bank, who you talk to. We think that belongs to you, and nobody else.

Elpis (ἐλπίς) is Greek for hope — the last thing left in the box.

Our promises

Six things we hold ourselves to. Short enough to remember, and easy to check.

No logs, no profiles

We answer your question and forget it. There's nothing to sell, and nothing to leak.

No one in the middle

Our own resolver asks the root servers directly. Your lookups never pass through Google or Cloudflare.

We block junk, not opinions

Ads, trackers, malware and phishing — and we publish the lists. No news, politics or countries get blocked.

Sealed on the way

DNS over HTTPS and TLS keep your lookups private between your device and us.

Easy to check

Our resolver, our lists and this website are open source. Test us with tools we don't control.

Honest about limits

Encrypted DNS hides which sites you look up, not the sites you then visit. It isn't a VPN.

No middlemen

Plenty of "private" DNS services encrypt your question, then quietly pass it on to a big provider anyway. Here's the difference.

Our own resolver

ΕΛΠΙΣ Resolver

To answer questions ourselves, we needed software that could. So we wrote it: a recursive resolver in portable C99, open source down to the cryptography. It's the second resolver in the world to check post-quantum ML-DSA-44 signatures, after Cloudflare — because the signatures collected today are the ones someone will try to forge later.
