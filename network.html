Built for South East Asia

Fast where you are

A few things we do so pages, videos and games load quickly here, not just somewhere far away.

ECS for faster video

a.hitoha.moe and b.hitoha.moe send EDNS Client Subnet: a shortened prefix of your address, like a /24 or a /56, never the full one. YouTube and other CDNs use it to pick the cache nearest you. No other endpoint passes yours on.

DNS64 for IPv6-only

No IPv4 on your network, like a VPS with only IPv6? DNS64 gives IPv4-only sites an address on our own NAT64 gateway, so you reach them anyway. Nothing to install: use 64.hitoha.moe.

Encrypted all the way

DNS over HTTPS and DNS over TLS on every endpoint. Your ISP sees that you're using DNS, but not what you look up.

More than one of everything

Each endpoint has several hostnames on more than one network. Add two or three to your device and it keeps working when one goes down.

Pick the endpoint for your region

Malaysia, Singapore or Indonesia, Standard or Family, DoH or DoT. We build the address for you.
