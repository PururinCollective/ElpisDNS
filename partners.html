Which one fits

a.hitoha.moe · Family

For schools, offices, cafés and homes with kids. Blocks ads, trackers, malware and phishing, plus adult sites, and turns on safe search. Sends ECS, so YouTube and other CDNs serve from the nearest cache.

b.hitoha.moe · Standard

The everyday pick, fast across Malaysian networks. Blocks ads, trackers, malware and phishing, and leaves everything else alone. Sends ECS too.

gg.hitoha.moe · Standard

Answers from Singapore, tuned for international routes. A good way out when local DNS starts blocking things. Same filter as b.hitoha.moe, IPv4 only.

How it works

Your name, live in four steps

No account and no fee. Your customers see your name in their settings, and we do the rest.

  1. Pick a server from the table above, and a name under your domain, like secure-dns.company.com.
  2. Point the name at it. Add an A record for the server's IPv4 address and an AAAA record for its IPv6 one. gg.hitoha.moe takes the A record only.
  3. Message us with the name and the server you picked. The contacts are below.
  4. We add your name to the server and get it a TLS certificate. Once that's done, your name answers DoH and DoT.
